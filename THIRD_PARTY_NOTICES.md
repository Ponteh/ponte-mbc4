# Third-party notices

Ponte MBC4 uses the following third-party dependency through its plug-in and
user-interface layers. No third-party code is used in `Source/DSP`.

## JUCE

JUCE 9.0.1 is reused from the PonteDSP monorepo or fetched from its official
repository by CMake. Distribution of commercial binaries must use a JUCE
licence compatible with the business model and comply with its terms.

## VST 3 SDK

The VST 3 SDK used by the JUCE wrapper is supplied through JUCE and reports SDK
version 3.8.0 in the generated module metadata. Observe the SDK licence and any
applicable VST trademark guidelines when distributing the plug-in.

McDSP software, binaries, assets and source code are not included. The internal
MC2000 research specification records only public documentation, independent
measurements and clean-room behavioural models.
